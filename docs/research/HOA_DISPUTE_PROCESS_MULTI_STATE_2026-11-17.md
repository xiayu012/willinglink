# Multi-state research for the homepage step guides

Reviewed 2026-11-17. Targeted review of relevant provisions and official explanations across **26 states**, not complete legal codes or a nationwide compliance opinion. Sources include HOA/condominium laws, general mediation laws and court-stage rules. Their coverage is different; this list does not imply 26 states mandate HOA IDR or authorize this provider. Proposed bills, inaccessible pages and unrelated search hits were excluded from the count.

## Editorial conclusions

- Do not present California IDR followed by ADR as a nationwide mandatory sequence.
- Internal complaints, enforcement hearings, neutral mediation and binding arbitration are distinct. The HOA retains its internal responsibilities; a private facilitator does not replace a required director, hearing body or approved program.
- The common service journey is: explain the issue and location, check the applicable procedure and participants, arrange the discussion, then document agreed next steps. This is a service overview, not a universal statutory minimum.
- Mandatory participation does not mean forced settlement. Our ADR description specifically concerns mediation, not binding arbitration.
- Avoid nationwide promises about deadlines, costs, confidentiality, enforceability, mediator eligibility or tolling. Formal notices, service, filings and any official-program requirements must be checked individually before proceeding. Email intake alone does not fulfill them.
- Keep public steps short. Put one brief optional scope note under each guide, with research retained here rather than a state-by-state sales-page wall.
