## Records and questions

Our mistakes belong in the record.

Your board’s journal records the discussions, decisions, and our actions—including problems and unfinished work.

Ask us anything

### What are we paying for?

Replies, owner conversations, proposals, records, and approved tasks. We do them. Owners keep using text and email. No FretGone accounts required.

### Who can read the journal?

Board members, without a Notion signup. We arrange access at setup. Owners’ private messages stay out of other owners’ view.

### What needs our approval?

Spending, orders, and other actions outside the limits we agree on. We confirm phone approvals at setup. Your HOA’s voting rules and board responsibilities still apply.

### Can we keep the tools we already use?

We check compatibility first. The setup uses a browser agent on an available board computer to work in your email, HOA, and accounting websites. You don’t have to sit at the screen.

### How do you protect the record?

Notion pages can be edited. AWS-backed archiving can help expose changes; availability and coverage must be confirmed before we promise that protection. We also explain data access, processing, and retention before connecting accounts.
