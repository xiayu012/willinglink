### 001

【编号】001

【来源作品】The Big Bang Theory（生活大爆炸）

【季/集】Season 3 Episode 22 — The Staircase Implementation

【来源类型】电视剧

【冲突类别】温度 / 规则制定

【参与人物】Leonard、Sheldon

【冲突背景】莱纳德觉得屋里冷，想把恒温器往上调两度；谢尔顿立刻搬出两人签过的“室友协议”，说这个温度是当初白纸黑字约定好的。争执焦点很快从“温度”滑向“室友协议到底能不能被推翻”。

【对白片段】

Leonard: It's two degrees, Sheldon. I just wanna turn up the thermostat two degrees.
Sheldon: Let me point out that two degrees can be the difference between water and steam.
Leonard: Yes, if we lived in a tea kettle.
Sheldon: This is the temperature you agreed to in the roommate agreement.
Leonard: Screw the roommate agreement.
Sheldon: No, you don't screw the roommate agreement, the roommate agreement screws you.
Leonard: You know what, go to hell and set their thermostat.
Sheldon: I don't have to go to hell. At 73 degrees, I'm there already.

【这个片段为什么有价值】典型室友恒温器纠纷，又叠加了“成文室友协议被引用、被反抗”的规则冲突；两人立场清晰、一进一退，短小完整，贴近合租里“温度/约定”这类高频摩擦。

### 002

【编号】002

【来源作品】The Big Bang Theory（生活大爆炸）

【季/集】Season 4 Episode 21 — The Agreement Dissection

【来源类型】电视剧

【冲突类别】卫生间 / 规则违反

【参与人物】Sheldon、Leonard、Priya

【冲突背景】谢尔顿在“紧急”情况下闯进莱纳德和女友正在使用的卫生间，并引用室友协议为自己开脱。莱纳德的女友 Priya 是律师，当场逐条拆解这份协议，把“紧急情况”的定义打成对莱纳德有利的歧义。

【对白片段】

Sheldon: What are you doing in there? She can't be in here. We were in here first.
Leonard: You can't be here.
Sheldon: According to the roommate agreement paragraph nine, subsection B: The right to bathroom privacy is suspended in the event of force majeure. And believe me, I'm experiencing a very majeure force.
Leonard: Oh, come on, you can't wait two minutes?
Sheldon: Dr. Leonard Hofstadter, you are officially charged with two violations of the roommate agreement.
Leonard: Do you waive reading of the charges? Yeah, fine.
Priya: Hang on, no. My client does not waive reading of the charges.
Sheldon: Proceed. Count the first: On or about the 28th day of April the accused did knowingly and with malice aforethought deny access to the shared bathroom in a time of emergency, to wit, my back teeth were floating.
Priya: Can I see the roommate agreement?
Sheldon: Um, it's fairly technical.
Priya: I think I can handle it. Based on a cursory reading, it doesn't look like you have much of a case, Sheldon. Section seven here on the right to enter in emergency situations is not specific as to what constitutes an emergency.
Sheldon: No, that's ridiculous. A bathroom emergency is self-explanatory.
Priya: Is it? If Leonard forgot to trim his nose hair could he barge in while you were showering?
Sheldon: Irrelevant. Leonard doesn't trim his nose hair.
Priya: The point is, Sheldon, the legal principle is ambiguity in a contract benefits the party that did not draft it. In this case, Leonard.

【这个片段为什么有价值】公共卫生间使用权的经典冲突，且把“室友协议/房屋规则”的模糊条款拿出来正面交锋；第三人（律师）逐条反驳，冲突结构完整，很适合作为规则协商类的多轮情景。

### 003

【编号】003

【来源作品】New Girl（杰茜驾到）

【季/集】Season 1 Episode 16 — Control

【来源类型】电视剧

【冲突类别】公共空间 / 物品使用 / 规则制定

【参与人物】Jess、Schmidt、Nick、Winston

【冲突背景】Jess 从街上捡回一个柜子（hutch）想放进客厅，控制欲极强的室友 Schmidt 坚决反对，认为“松木不属于这间 loft”。Jess 用“我也付了房租，有权利添东西”回击；Nick 和 Winston 则点出这个家其实一直由 Schmidt 主导清洁和秩序。

【对白片段】

Jess: You believe I found this on the street?
Schmidt: Who would want to throw this away? A blind man who recovered his sight? Get rid of it, Jess. Pine has no place in this loft. The wood of poor people and outhouses.
Jess: I pay rent. So I think that gives me the right to add a few things.
Schmidt: Jess, you are wrong. Okay? My answer is no.
Jess: Well, my answer is yes. I'm keeping the hutch.

Jess: Gosh! Schmidt makes me so mad! I want to put one thing, one thing in the living room, and he won't let me.
Nick: We live in a fragile ecosystem. It's a delicate balance.
Jess: It's a broken ecosystem. Dominated by an apex predator.
Winston: He can't handle it when things aren't clean and orderly. It's been that way since the day he moved in.

Schmidt: I'm gonna assume that these are your spices jammed in here with my spices. I'd appreciate it if your non-Tahitian vanilla was not touching my Tahitian vanilla.
Jess: Oh, Schmidt. They're more than touching.
Schmidt: Where's my burr coffee grinder?
Jess: I think it's in the hutch.
Schmidt: You put it in the hutch? This thing was on the street.
Jess: It's a coffee grinder, Schmidt. I think it'll be okay.

【这个片段为什么有价值】围绕“谁有权往公共空间添东西”展开，从家具到香料到厨房用具层层升级；包含强势室友的单方否决、被否决方的“我付了房租”反抗，以及第三方对房屋生态的旁白，非常贴近合租中“公共区域谁说了算”的真实摩擦。

【来源类型】电视剧

Schmidt: Me.

Winston: I need a win. Give me the spot, or I'll kill you all!
Jess: Guys, we all have crappy parking. Why don't we just all share the spot? We'll each get it for a day and a half every week, and then we split Sunday into six-hour shifts.
Schmidt: Does it say "Share stuff" in the Constitution of America? No, it does not. Nicholas, what does it say?
Nick: Don't share stuff.
Schmidt: No one deserves that spot more than I do. 'Cause Lord knows, I've done my fair share of spotworthy things.
Winston: I've lived in this loft as long, if not longer, than anyone. I have the crummiest room, I'm on 24-hour grout watch.
Schmidt: I pay the lion's share of the utilities, do I not?

Schmidt: It's WiFi.

Schmidt: Don't play the lady card.
Jess: I will play the lady card.
Nick: Race card.
Winston: Lazy drunk card.
Schmidt: Deserves-the-spot card.

【这个片段为什么有价值】四人对一个稀缺公共资源（停车位）的争夺，从“共享提议”被否，到各自摆资格、贴标签、翻旧账，完整呈现多方协调失败后的僵局；对白自然、立场鲜明，是“多人抢同一资源”的高价值样本。

【季/集】Season 2 Episode 16 — The Cushion Saturation

【来源类型】电视剧

【冲突类别】公共空间 / 规则制定

【参与人物】Sheldon、Penny、Leonard

【冲突背景】客厅沙发有一个位置是谢尔顿长年认定的“我的位置”，任何人坐到那里都会被他要求让开。佩妮只是临时坐了一下就被纠正，莱纳德则想让他“灵活一点”，结果反而触发谢尔顿把座位、书桌椅子的用途分得清清楚楚。

Penny: Are you planning on sitting here?
Sheldon: No, I'm going to the comic-book store.
Penny: What difference does it make?

Sheldon: I'm not unhappy.

Leonard: Why don't you just eat in your desk chair?

### 011

【编号】011

【来源作品】The Big Bang Theory（生活大爆炸）

【季/集】Season 3 Episode 22 — The Staircase Implementation

【来源类型】电视剧

【冲突类别】搬进 / 规则制定 / 卫生间

【参与人物】Sheldon、Leonard

【冲突背景】莱纳德上门看房，谢尔顿像面试一样考他科学问题和科幻偏好，又提前把客厅座位设为“永久占座”，还要求新室友固定“排便时间表”，排到几点就是几点。莱纳德为了住进这间租金合适的公寓，一路配合这些奇葩条款。

【对白片段】

Leonard: I'm Leonard Hofstadter. I called about the apartment.
Sheldon: What is the sixth noble gas?
Leonard: Radon?
Sheldon: Are you asking me or telling me?
Leonard: Telling you. Telling you.
Sheldon: All right. Next question. Kirk or Picard?
Leonard: Well, that's tricky. Original series over Next Generation, but Picard over Kirk.
Sheldon: Correct. You've passed the first barrier to roommatehood. You may enter.

Sheldon: No, that's where I sit.
