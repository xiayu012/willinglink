### 001

【编号】001

【来源作品】The Big Bang Theory（生活大爆炸）

【季/集】Season 3 Episode 22 — The Staircase Implementation

【来源类型】电视剧

【冲突类别】温度 / 规则制定

【参与人物】Leonard、Sheldon

【冲突背景】莱纳德觉得屋里冷，想把恒温器往上调两度；谢尔顿立刻搬出两人签过的“室友协议”，说这个温度是当初白纸黑字约定好的。争执焦点很快从“温度”滑向“室友协议到底能不能被推翻”。

【对白片段】

Leonard: It's two degrees, Sheldon. I just wanna turn up the thermostat two degrees.
Sheldon: Let me point out that two degrees can be the difference between water and steam.
Leonard: Yes, if we lived in a tea kettle.
Sheldon: This is the temperature you agreed to in the roommate agreement.
Leonard: Screw the roommate agreement.
Sheldon: No, you don't screw the roommate agreement, the roommate agreement screws you.
Leonard: You know what, go to hell and set their thermostat.
Sheldon: I don't have to go to hell. At 73 degrees, I'm there already.

【这个片段为什么有价值】典型室友恒温器纠纷，又叠加了“成文室友协议被引用、被反抗”的规则冲突；两人立场清晰、一进一退，短小完整，贴近合租里“温度/约定”这类高频摩擦。

【来源类型】电视剧

【来源类型】电视剧

【冲突类别】卫生间 / 规则违反

【参与人物】Sheldon、Leonard、Priya

【冲突背景】谢尔顿在“紧急”情况下闯进莱纳德和女友正在使用的卫生间，并引用室友协议为自己开脱。莱纳德的女友 Priya 是律师，当场逐条拆解这份协议，把“紧急情况”的定义打成对莱纳德有利的歧义。

Sheldon: What are you doing in there? She can't be in here. We were in here first.

Leonard: Do you waive reading of the charges? Yeah, fine.
Priya: Hang on, no. My client does not waive reading of the charges.
Sheldon: Proceed. Count the first: On or about the 28th day of April the accused did knowingly and with malice aforethought deny access to the shared bathroom in a time of emergency, to wit, my back teeth were floating.
Priya: Can I see the roommate agreement?
Sheldon: Um, it's fairly technical.
Priya: I think I can handle it. Based on a cursory reading, it doesn't look like you have much of a case, Sheldon. Section seven here on the right to enter in emergency situations is not specific as to what constitutes an emergency.

【来源类型】电视剧

【参与人物】Jess、Schmidt、Nick、Winston

【冲突背景】Jess 从街上捡回一个柜子（hutch）想放进客厅，控制欲极强的室友 Schmidt 坚决反对，认为“松木不属于这间 loft”。Jess 用“我也付了房租，有权利添东西”回击；Nick 和 Winston 则点出这个家其实一直由 Schmidt 主导清洁和秩序。

Schmidt: Jess, you are wrong. Okay? My answer is no.
Jess: Well, my answer is yes. I'm keeping the hutch.

Jess: Gosh! Schmidt makes me so mad! I want to put one thing, one thing in the living room, and he won't let me.
Nick: We live in a fragile ecosystem. It's a delicate balance.
Jess: It's a broken ecosystem. Dominated by an apex predator.
Winston: He can't handle it when things aren't clean and orderly. It's been that way since the day he moved in.

Schmidt: I'm gonna assume that these are your spices jammed in here with my spices. I'd appreciate it if your non-Tahitian vanilla was not touching my Tahitian vanilla.
Jess: Oh, Schmidt. They're more than touching.
Schmidt: Where's my burr coffee grinder?
Jess: I think it's in the hutch.
